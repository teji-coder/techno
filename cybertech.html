Cybertech

The team behind BantayBizz.

Cybertech is a student startup team focused on creating simple, practical, and user-friendly cybersecurity solutions for small businesses.

🎯

Mission

To empower small businesses with smart and proactive cybersecurity solutions that protect digital transactions, prevent financial loss, and detect cyber threats.

🌍

Vision

To become a trusted leader in innovative and user-friendly cybersecurity solutions for safer and smarter digital businesses worldwide.

Why We Created BantayBizz

Because small businesses deserve protection too.

BantayBizz was created after recognizing that many local sellers, vendors, freelancers, and small business owners rely on digital payments every day, but often lack accessible cybersecurity tools. Cybertech wanted to design a solution that feels simple, affordable, and helpful even for users with limited technical knowledge.

Through BantayBizz, the team aims to help business owners prevent scams, secure transactions, detect suspicious activities, and build customer trust through a Certified Secure experience.
