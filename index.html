About the Startup

Smart cybersecurity made simple for small businesses.

BantayBizz is a mobile cybersecurity application created to help micro, small, and medium businesses protect their daily digital transactions. It is built for vendors, online sellers, freelancers, home-based businesses, delivery services, and startups that rely on mobile wallets such as GCash, Maya, and other online payment platforms.

The app runs quietly in the background while checking suspicious activities, scam attempts, spyware risks, unusual payments, and possible digital fraud. With easy alerts, security tips, emergency lock tools, and a Certified Secure badge, BantayBizz helps business owners build customer trust without needing advanced technical knowledge.
